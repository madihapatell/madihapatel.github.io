Hi! I'm Madiha, a content writer who cares about two things at once: writing that sounds like a real person, and writing that gets found on Google.

I'm currently a Content Writer at StyleSpeak Magazine. Before that I wrote SEO-optimised content across e-commerce, beauty and home-furnishing brands. That covered long-form blogs, keyword-targeted category pages, Google Search Ads copy, Meta Ads, product listings, newsletters, press releases and brand video scripts. I like working closely with design, 3D and listing teams, and I like seeing an idea all the way from brief to published.

Off the clock, I'm an introvert and a foodie who loves dressing up and trying new things. I'm slowly building a food, travel and lifestyle page on Instagram. You'll find both sides of me on the blog.
