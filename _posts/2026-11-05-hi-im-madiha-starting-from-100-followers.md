Okay, real talk. I have about 100 followers, and I've decided I want to become a full-time food, travel and lifestyle creator.

I can hear my inner introvert screaming. Let's do it anyway.

#### So, who am I?

I'm Madiha. I live in Bandra West, Mumbai, and I escape to Pune every now and then. I'm an introvert who somehow loves dressing up. I'm a foodie who will happily cross the city for one dessert. And I'm always looking for a new experience, as long as it doesn't involve too many people talking to me at once.

By day I'm a content writer. So yes, I write captions for a living, and I still overthink my own.

#### Why start now, with *100* followers?

Because waiting until I feel "ready" was never going to happen. Every creator I love started somewhere. I'd rather you watch me figure it out than show up one day pretending I always knew what I was doing.

Here's what I want this to become:

- **A place for honest recommendations.** That means cafes, food spots, weekend plans and stays I'd actually send to a friend.
- **Something that feels like me.** Soft, a little funny and warm, not a list of "top 10 cafes" you've already seen 400 times.
- **A record of the journey.** That includes growing from 100 followers, learning to shoot and edit, and the behind-the-scenes nobody posts.

#### Who this is for

If you're somewhere between 20 and 35, living in Mumbai or Pune, and your weekends usually start with *"where should we go?"*, hi, this is for you. It's for date ideas (romantic *and* friendly), solo plans for when you need a day with yourself, and aesthetic cafes worth the trip.

#### Why a blog too?

Reels are great, but 30 seconds is never enough. This blog is where the longer version lives: what I ordered, what was worth it, what wasn't, and the little details that don't fit in a caption.

If any of this sounds like your kind of thing, come along. I'll be on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/_.madiha19/) too, probably overthinking my next caption.
