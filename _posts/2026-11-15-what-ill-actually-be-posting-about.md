Every "how to grow on Instagram" video says the same thing: *pick your content pillars.* So I sat down and actually did it. Here's what you can expect from me, and why.

#### 1. Food

This one was never in question. I'll be eating my way through:

- **Cafes**, especially the cosy, aesthetic ones you want to sit in for three hours
- **Restaurants** worth dressing up for
- **Hidden gems** that don't show up on every list
- **Desserts**, because this is non-negotiable
- **Well-known street food**, done properly
- **Food experiences**, the ones that are more than just a meal

You'll get honest reviews. If something was overhyped, I'll say so, nicely.

#### 2. Travel

Not the "quit my job and backpack for a year" kind (yet). More like:

- **Weekend trips** you can plan on a Thursday
- **Staycations** for when you need a break without the airport
- **Solo travel**, introvert-approved
- **Road trips**
- **Trekking and camping**, for when I'm feeling brave

#### 3. Lifestyle

This is the soft, slow, "romanticise your life" corner:

- **Day in my life** in Mumbai, and sometimes Pune
- **Soft life** and little things that make an ordinary day feel good
- **Dating experiences**, the date ideas and the stories behind them
- **Self-growth**, because I'm figuring things out too

#### 4. The creator journey

This is the part I'm most nervous about and most excited for. I'm going to share what it's actually like to build a page from about 100 followers: learning to shoot and edit, what's working, what flopped, and the behind-the-scenes.

#### Where to find it

Reels, carousels and stories on Instagram. Pins on Pinterest. Eventually longer videos on YouTube. And the full, unhurried version of all of it here on the blog.

If you're a Mumbai or Pune person who's always hunting for the next weekend plan, I think we'll get along.
